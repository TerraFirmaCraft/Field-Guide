漿果叢

群巒生活模組新增了一些漿果叢。關於野生葡萄藤的資訊，請參閱釀酒指南。

龍葵

First is nightshade. Nightshade is a poisonous berry. When put into soup, it makes poisonous Stinky Soup. It is found between 200-400mm of rain and 7-24 C temperature in forests. It bears fruit in the month of August, flowering the month prior.

菠蘿

Pineapple bushes are found 250-500mm of rainfall and 20-32 C temperature in forests. They bear fruit in the month of September, flowering the two months prior. Pineapples are like any other fruit, except that they can be made into Pineapple Leather.

曬乾的菠蘿能被製成菠蘿纖維。

菠蘿線是透過使用紡錘與菠蘿纖維合成得到的。

最後，菠蘿布能在織機中被加工。它是普通皮革的植物替代品，可用於裝飾、合成或其他用途！
